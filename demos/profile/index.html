About

I teach the way I wish I had been taught: work the problem slowly, out loud, until the student can explain it back to me. No shortcuts, no memorised formulas without the reasoning underneath.

Groups are capped at four because five is where the quiet student stops asking. Parents get a short written note after every month — what we covered, what is not sticking yet.
